<!-- feature-section -->
## Colour tools for Twig

Take a colour stored in Craft and derive the variants your template needs at render time. A single source value can produce hover states, tints, shades, translucent overlays, or a complementary accent.
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-grid -->
- :icon[sun-high] **Lighten and darken** Derive brighter or deeper variants by a controlled amount.
- :icon[droplet-half-2] **Tint and shade** Blend towards white or black for a related colour scale.
- :icon[blend-mode] **Mix colours** Combine two values to create a deliberate intermediate colour.
- :icon[droplet] **Fade values** Adjust opacity and return a value suitable for translucent CSS.
- :icon[color-swatch] **Saturation controls** Saturate, desaturate or convert a value to greyscale by a chosen amount.
- :icon[color-picker] **Complementary colours** Derive the colour opposite an existing value without maintaining a second field.
<!-- feature-grid-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Common colour formats

Convert between Hex, HSL, RGB, RGBA, OKLCH, CMYK and related representations before passing the result to CSS or another template operation. Helpers can also determine whether a colour is broadly light or dark for contrast decisions.
<!-- feature-section-end -->
